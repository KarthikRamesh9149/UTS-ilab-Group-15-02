OpenHands, the registered custom variants and bounded diagnostic block. Select
and freeze the custom implementation before any final scoring. Then run fresh
final evaluations on all 89 tasks for Terminus-2, all 89 for OpenHands, and all
89 for the single frozen custom finalist: 267 final cells, including 178 baseline
cells. The six existing development attempts do not fill any final cell.

No final-outcome tuning, baseline selection after results, variant stitching or
selective reruns are permitted. Primary success still means more official
verifier passes than a preselected established comparator on the full matched
89-task evaluation. An efficiency finding is not assignment success without
mentor acceptance, and zero-pass comparisons are not meaningful efficiency
wins. This amendment authorises a bounded attempt to complete the study; it
does not guarantee sufficient remaining credit, a passing score or a custom win.
