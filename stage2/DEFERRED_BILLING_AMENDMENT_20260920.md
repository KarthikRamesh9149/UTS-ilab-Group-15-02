# Continue collection with conservatively reserved, unresolved billing

On 20 September 2026 the user explicitly directed: "dont stop over unresolved
provider charge and all that", and prioritised all 89 Terminus-2 and all 89
OpenHands tasks. This prospective amendment supersedes the earlier rule that
only two named historical unknowns could be retained. It does not change or
backdate the original evidence or claim that the earlier rules passed.

## Collection and accounting are separate

A terminal attempt may have an official binary verifier result while its API
bill remains uncertain. After model access is revoked and all owned containers,
networks and volumes are confirmed removed, the trusted host may register a
billing deferral bound to the immutable original result and gateway evidence.
An active request, unfinished attempt or failed cleanup cannot be deferred.

The original request row is never rewritten into a receipt. Unknown charges
stay unknown with their full maximum reservation held. A settled response whose
independent receipt is missing retains its recorded charge, with the difference
up to its full reservation also held. All such exposure counts against the
original project/stage/setup caps and fresh account/key availability before the
next request. The US$2 reserve, equal estimated trial allowance and all official
task deadlines remain unchanged. This grants no top-up or larger allowance.

Only a new, scheduled attempt may proceed. A failed scored task is never
replayed, renamed, replaced or omitted. Within an active attempt an unresolved
dispatch still prevents another request; this amendment does not enable blind
retries. Conflicting receipts, demonstrated overcharges, invalid protocol or
evidence, and unsafe teardown remain real faults requiring correction.

The prior two historical registrations and all original result bytes remain
unchanged. Future deferrals use the same standing rule, not a new discretionary
exception each time. Fresh live qualification must still demonstrate actual
model/tool operation: a rate-limited or failed fixture is not a qualification
pass just because its financial exposure is reserved.

## Reporting and fair comparisons

Keep official verifier failures in their original condition/task denominators.
Report billing verification coverage and known charges separately from reserved
liabilities. Unknown costs/tokens are null, never zero; complete accuracy does
not imply complete financial evidence. No cost/token efficiency claim is valid
while either compared condition has incomplete financial/usage coverage.

When development accuracy ties and any competing condition has incomplete cost
evidence, omit the cost tie-break for every competing condition symmetrically;
retain the remaining registered tie-break order. This is declared before custom
development has started, not chosen after observing a preferred custom outcome.

## Prioritise the complete baselines

After native integration and the initial fixed-20 systemic checks, run all 178
fresh baseline cells before custom development. Their registration must precede
dispatch and bind the canonical task IDs, limits, model, admission and sources.
They are the baseline portion of the same 267-cell final comparison, not extra
scoring or a replacement for the 89 frozen-custom cells. The six existing
development outcomes remain development only. Custom design/selection may use
only the fixed 20-task development subset; the other baseline task outcomes
must not guide custom changes. Preserve the exact baseline registration when
assembling the final matrix, with no replay or stale-source substitution.

Neither a standing financial deferral nor passing software tests guarantees
sufficient credit for every remaining task, a high accuracy or a custom win.
Execution and actual outcomes must be reported separately from this amendment.

Spending PDFs remain paused. This amendment changes runtime accounting and
technical result handling only; it does not authorise report generation.
